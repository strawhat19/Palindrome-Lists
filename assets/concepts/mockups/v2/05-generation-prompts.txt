Hero ends around y470. Keep only these three elements and eyebrow in hero.

Show ONLY TWO palindrome cards total in one generous two-column row with subtle thin borders and 10px radius, NO heavy shadows. Card 1 badge "Word" and large "level". Card 2 badge "Phrase" and large "Never odd or even.". Every card has a small discreet "Details" disclosure with chevron, CLOSED. This is where source, author and dates will live. All five must be visible. Only vote and comment counts, no heart total, NO repeated explanatory labels. Bookmark and heart distinct. Don't omit downvote. No avatars or status chips.

Quiet footer at page bottom "© 2026 Palindrome Lists" left, "About  Terms  Privacy  Contact" center, "Piratechs ↗" right, subtle hairline. A discreet scroll-up button at bottom right can be small.
Avoid all extra stats, four-card grids, tables, testimonial panels, pricing, graphic decoration, gradients, texture, browser chrome, device frames, watermarks, stock photos, oversized buttons, metadata columns and duplication. This is a simplified light landing page, not a full dashboard. Keep spelling and natural card proportions.
Color direction "Soft Blue": Light near-white #F8FAFD background, white cards, muted azure #4776DB accent, subtle light-blue #EBF1FC selected filters, almost black #18202B text. Header/logo and hero accent muted blue. This retains the preferred white/blue aesthetic with a much calmer color and fewer panels.
